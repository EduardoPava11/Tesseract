## 1. What is now true, and settled

### The directory: a file's job is its address

Three organising ideas were fighting in one tree. Ten job-named
directories now: `Sense Signal Solve Learn Store Edit Weave Meter Law
Surface`. `Store/` is new and is the point: persistence had been spread
across three directories that did not know about each other, plus a
struct buried in `GIFMachine.swift`. UserDefaults is the only
required-reason API this app declares, and a privacy declaration is
auditable exactly when every writer of it shares an address.

One invariant got stronger by the move: the primitive drawing-vocabulary
allowlist used to be a hand-kept name list including one file from a
different directory. It is now `Surface/Cells/`. **The allowlist is a
directory, so it cannot drift from itself.**

### The ANE encodes and compresses the signal

`spec/neural/TensorEncoder.hs` TA1-TA9, `nn/tensor-codec/`,
`Store/CaptureTensor.swift` + `Store/TensorANE.swift`, 11 tests.

**TA1 is the unlock and it is a small piece of algebra.** The 2x2x2
spacetime pool FACTORS into a temporal pair-average and a spatial 2x2
average, and the two commute. Fold frame pairs into the channel axis and
the temporal half is a 1x1 convolution; the spatial half is a stock
avg_pool. So the octave transform needs no 3D operator, which is the
thing the engine schedules worst. One dispatch, 0.9 ms on M3 Max.

**TA3 makes fp16 safe by arithmetic rather than by hope.** A flipped
sign costs exactly `2*min(|r|, g)`, not `2g`, and a flip can only happen
where |r| is under the engine's resolution. Measured twice: 98.30% and
97.14% sign agreement, worst disagreeing residual 4.85e-4 and 4.39e-4
against an fp16 ulp of 4.88e-4. **Every disagreement is a residual the
engine could not see.**

Measured size: 1,099,586 B against CubeStore's 1,835,024 B.

### The colour latent has an exact arity

`spec/neural/ColourLatent.hs` CL1-CL8. A GIF's 49152 palette bytes are
generated by 16 fields per frame, TWO of which are Bools, so **896
numbers + 128 bits, 54.86 : 1, exact.** I got this wrong twice before
getting it right, which is recorded in the file.

**CL6 is the finding.** The mean-step/variance-step ratio is strictly
decreasing and CROSSES ONE (5.15 down to 0.66). Their order reverses, so
no positive constant weight makes them commensurable. The metric must be
a FIELD. Whether that field needs weights is open: the pullback J^T J
through the continuous table is arithmetic.

### S and K are parallel rails, and memory colour is what weights are for

`spec/neural/MemoryColour.hs` MC1-MC10.

A 14-agent workflow refuted "SK terms as a query language" with a
measurement, and it refuted the right thing: those terms compose S and K
IN SERIES, as a cursor on a ladder. Daniel meant them IN PARALLEL. Two
rails. K contracts (scene to Gaussian, measurement), S expands (Gaussian
to 256 entries, rendering). The edit surface is that pair and the model's
I/O is that pair.

**And the thing between them is the only place weights belong.**
Bartleson (JOSA 50(1) 73, 1960): ten familiar objects, fifty observers,
931 Munsell chips, and ALL TEN memory colours differed significantly
from the natural ones, shifted toward each object's dominant chroma.
Where a human remembers a colour is not a consequence of anything. It is
a measurement of people, and that is what a model can hold.

The types already line up: `DyadPalette.Stats` is already a centroid
plus a 3x3 covariance in OKLab, which is what MCRI models a memory
colour with. 14 numbers in, 14 numbers out.

### The tree, and useful energy

`spec/neural/MerkleSearch.hs` MT1-MT12. KataGo's shape: position = a GIF
as its canonical generator, move = an expansion S, transposition table =
the Merkle hash for free, policy head = where to look, value head = the
energy shown to the user.

**MT9, the economy: the analogy inverts in one place.** KataGo must
learn its value head because Go has no closed form for a non-terminal
position. This app has one. So the value head needs no weights and
**only the policy is learned.**

**MT6/MT7/MT8, useful energy, measured on TilingEntropy's own probes:**

```
frame       E (bits)    E_wall
balanced        0.00    1573.06
random          0.00      82.64
face         4182.21    8064.00
neel        28672.00    8064.00
solid       32768.00    8064.00
```

Grey is the CEILING of E, not the floor, so descending E structurally
cannot go grey. That is the opposite of descending a distortion. But E
alone cannot separate balanced from random static, and E_wall alone
cannot separate a face from grey. **Together they exclude both by
DOMINATION, not tuning:** solid is strictly dominated by face at equal
E_wall, random is strictly dominated by balanced at equal E. The front
is exactly {balanced, face}. A scalar would have had to pick a corner.

## 2. THE GAPS, which are the frontier

Ordered by what unblocks the most exploration.

### G1. Nothing has been walked. Zero device evidence.

Every number in this document is from a Mac or a simulator, on synthetic
fixtures. The device pass has been owed since 2026-08-11 and nothing
here reduces it. **The first exploration is a real capture through this
pipeline**, because several findings below are explicitly flagged as
possibly optimistic on real data.

### G2. The tree has never been searched.

MT1-MT12 prove the tree is well formed, finite, addressable and
monotone. **No search has been run on it.** There is no Swift port, no
policy, and no node has ever been expanded outside a Haskell axiom. This
is the single largest unexplored thing and it is the next session's
subject.

What it needs, in order: `Meter/TilingEnergy.swift` (E and E_wall from
an emitted cube, gated against the six pinned probes, zero weights),
then a search that expands and keeps the front, then a policy.

### G3. The zerotree's saving may not survive real sensor noise.

Measured: a noiseless cube gives two phases and 24.9% loud; the same
cube with a 0.0015 noise floor gives ONE phase, so CT7's own branch pays
flat and stores 110 KB of noise signs. The 95.3%-quiet figure the
zerotree was ruled on is optimistic for any real capture. Donoho MAD
thresholding is an equally derived alternative that kills 27.9% for
+0.9% rmse. **Not taken. This is a ruling and it wants real data.**

### G4. There is no trajectory law.

Every colour law is about ONE frame. The artifact is 64 of them and the
GIF loops forever. Nothing anywhere describes how consecutive ticks may
relate, and two rulings block it: whether a non-causal temporal filter
may exist at all (WS5 says frame t reads only frames <= t), and whether
a rotation of Z/64 is an equivalence on the artifact. **Paths are
literally the thing that does not exist yet**, which is why the next
session is about them.

### G5. The memory anchors are a stand-in.

MC's section 3 obeys Bartleson's measured DIRECTION, not his
coordinates. Every axiom quantifies over the anchor set, so real values
change data and no law. This needs the 1960 paper and a Munsell-to-OKLab
conversion. **No training run, just the numbers.**

### G6. Three live defects, verified, untouched.

- `DyadEnergy.eDist = w + sOcc` sums a dimensionless wall fraction with
  a normalised entropy. TE8 says in as many words that these are
  different currencies and must not be summed. **It rides every exported
  GIF as provenance.**
- `TemporalLoop.hs` is a false green: zero axioms, zero gates, passes
  `make test` on exit code alone. The harness needs a third clause.
- Three SKGene mlpackages are bundled, loaded and **never given a
  prediction**. The only three `.prediction(` call sites are ANELoop,
  DyadANE and TensorANE.

### G7. The ANE does almost nothing in the shipped app.

`DyadAssign` is superseded and uncalled. `ANELoop` is behind
`phaseChaosLoop = false`. `TensorEncode` has no call site. `jepaH` is
false. The only ANE work on any user path is the SKGene provenance
trace, and per G6 those models are not even predicted. **Seven
mlpackages ship and effectively none runs.**

### G8. The Haskell reorganisation is designed and not done.

The workflow produced a full 74-file manifest into 12 job directories
plus `Registry.hs` (RG0-RG10) making spec-to-port correspondence
machine-checked. Not executed, because Ruling 4 changes what lands in
it. `docs/workflow-2026-08-14-sk-energy-registry.md` holds the manifest.

### G9. Nothing reaches a user surface.

`Reweave` has no non-test caller. The edit tick loop does not exist.
Everything built this session is a law with a test, not a feature.
