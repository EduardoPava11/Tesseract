## 1. The theorem that makes this a completion, not a rewrite

The 8-bit index already carries the whole structure:

- **Pairing levels.** For ℓ ∈ {0..7}, the sibling map s_ℓ(i) = i XOR 2^ℓ
  is an involution; the maps commute and generate (ℤ/2)⁸. Pairs,
  pairs-of-pairs, pairs-of-pairs-of-pairs — every scale of pairing is
  one subset of levels.
- **σ is the total pairing.** The DYAD involution partner(i) = 255 − i
  = i XOR 255 = s₀∘s₁∘…∘s₇. Pairing pairs *all the way up* IS the
  involution the table law has carried since DY1. Nothing breaks; the
  hierarchy was latent in the arithmetic.
- **Three pairings = the atom.** Any three levels compose to 2×2×2 = 8:1.
  Canonical stratification (LSB-first): levels {0,1,2} then {3,4,5}
  then {6,7} — quotients **256 → 32 → 4**: two full 8:1 octaves with
  **4 anchors** at the top (the tesseract's own 4-epoch echo; 4⁴ = 256).
- **The binomial ladder is the tree's shadow.** [1,1,2,4,8,16,32,64]
  = the growth rings of a balanced binary tree on the 128 figures
  (2^(k−1) new nodes per split generation). The "strict binomial
  distribution" is preserved as the tree's level-size law — it was
  always a flattened tree.

On the **figure half** (7 bits, since grounds are generated by the v7
same-hue ground law): levels {0..6}, strata {0,1,2} {3,4,5} {6} —
**128 = 8 · 8 · 2**. The σ half inherits the identical tree through
i ↦ 255 − i (complement maps siblings to siblings), so every pairing
scale exists simultaneously in figure space, ground space, and across
the dyad.

## 2. Grounded in latent algorithms (replacing hand geometry)

Today's solver places figures on **designed** rings (fixed radii
ρ_k = 2k/7, fixed angles 2πj/n) in the PC1×PC2 plane. The pair tree
replaces designed geometry with **latent splits**:

- **v1 — the exact latent algorithm (no NN, no constants):**
  hierarchical principal-direction splitting. A node holds a weighted
  sample set; split along the node's own top eigenvector at its
  weighted mean; recurse 7 generations → 128 leaves. Every parameter
  is an eigenvector or a mean of the capture's own staged
  distribution. RL4's allocation law (n ∝ √λ) is satisfied *locally
  at every node*, and balanced splits reproduce the binomial level
  sizes exactly. Node centroids are exact weighted means, so
  **parent = mean of children** at every level — the palette's own
  2×2×2 ↔ 1 pooling, byte-auditable.
- **v2 — the trained model (the capture-assist NN):** distill v1.
  The network learns the *algorithm's fixed points*, exactly the
  SKGene grounding precedent (G1–G6): input = the rung-16 slow state
  (9 staged stats + background moments — the 5 Hz state DyadPreview
  already computes), output = the 127 split parameters (direction +
  offset per node). Training corpora are **synthetic GIF89a
  statistical variance** (the ★NO-CAPTURE-TRAINING decree: the
  sampler program, not captures); eval synthetic; device feel is the
  only real gate. Lab: `nn/pair-tree/` (MLX → coremltools →
  mlpackage, parity harness like nn/dyad-assign).

Why a model at all ("help the capture"): the exact algorithm costs a
full eigen-recursion per rung-16 tick; the distilled model is one ANE
dispatch, warm-startable (predict tree *delta* from previous tree +
new stats), and its hierarchical form gives log-time assignment (§4).

## 3. The 8:1 ratio in ALL the scales — the rung↔level alignment

The pair tree makes the palette's scale axis commensurate with the
cube's spatial rungs, both generated by the same atom:

| Spacetime (per rung, 2×2×2↔1) | Palette (per stratum, 3 pairings) |
|---|---|
| 64³ cube (full) | 256 leaves (8 bits) |
| 32³ (8:1) | 32 octet-parents (5 bits) |
| 16³ (64:1) | 4 anchors (2 bits) |

**Prefix law:** truncating ℓ low bits of an index = quantizing
against the level-ℓ centroids. So the chaos blur's rung-16 pooled
targets (S4 of the rate ladder) quantize against the **32-color
level**, not the full 256 — the background's palette rate drops 8:1
exactly when its spatial rate does. Joint (pixels × palette) rate
descends the SAME ladder; the RATE LEDGER's K̂ measures the win.

## 4. Assignment becomes a descent (the ANE shape)

Nearest-of-128 becomes **7 binary decisions** (or three 8-way stages:
octet → octet → pair): log-time VQ, ideal for a small fused ANE graph
(fixed iteration count — the reference_ane_performance law: fuse
fixed-iteration graphs, one dispatch). Greedy tree descent equals
exact nearest only under separation; disagreements are near-ties, and
the XP2 bounded-perturbation law already makes near-tie flips lawful —
the same posture that placed DyadAssign on the ANE. CPU exact search
stays the reference; parity gates stay.
